## Goal

Restore and maintain the car ahead of a planned supercharger build.

## Job 1: Propshaft replacement

**Why:** The car had a shudder and vibration under acceleration, the center carrier bearing was worn, and the flex disc showed dry rot and cracking. I ruled out the mounts and axle before settling on the propshaft.

**Work:** Removed the full exhaust, the O2 sensors and the dogbone mount, then installed a new propshaft. All torque-to-yield bolts were replaced and torqued to spec.

## Job 2: Valve cover gasket and PCV

**Why:** Oil leak from the valve cover and a whistling PCV system.

**Work:** Put the car in service position, removed the intake manifold, and replaced the valve cover gasket, PCV system and intake manifold gasket. The engine and transmission mounts were done at the same time.

**Problem:** The intake manifold on this engine takes a long time to remove. The service manual calls for shifting the radiators and crash bar forward 3 inches for clearance.

**Result:** Oil leak fixed, PCV system running smoothly, and a 5–7 mpg gain (measured tank to tank).
